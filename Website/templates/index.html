    Warnings

      About this model — and its limits

      What it does

      A MobileNetV2 network pre-trained on ImageNet is fine-tuned on 16 classes built from eight fruits × two conditions. Fruit identity and condition are read out of the same softmax: the eight "Fresh" class probabilities are summed into the fresh score, and the eight "Rotten" probabilities into the rotten score.

      What it cannot do

      • Shelf life. No capture dates, storage temperatures or days-to-spoilage labels exist in the dataset, so no number of days can be predicted.
      • Cross-sections. Only a small, unlabelled fraction of the dataset shows cut fruit, so cut-fruit analysis is not validated.
      • Food safety. A photograph cannot prove a fruit is safe to eat.
      • Other fruits. Fruits outside the eight supported types may be forced into a supported class; the confidence gate flags weak predictions, but it is not a guarantee.
